## 5. Safety Notice

The project relates to modifications inside mains powered electronic musical instruments.

Even though the display circuit itself operates at low voltage, installation may require opening an instrument that contains hazardous mains voltage and charged capacitors.

Work on mains powered equipment can cause electric shock, fire, injury, death, or damage to the instrument.

Only qualified persons with suitable knowledge of electrical safety, soldering, ESD handling, and vintage electronic equipment should perform the installation or any related modification.

Disconnect the instrument from mains power before opening it. Do not rely on the power switch alone. Allow sufficient time for capacitors to discharge before touching internal circuits.
